 BACKGROUND:
  The Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope is a next-generation visible and
  near-infrared wavelength space-based observatory. The mission lifetime is
  at least five years. It can be refueled in-flight robotically.

  It launched 2026-Aug-30 11:26:04 UTC on a Falcon Heavy from Kennedy Space
  Center (LC-39A) and will orbit the Earth-Sun L2 point.

  Objectives:
   * Search for extra-solar planets using gravitational microlensing
   * Probe chronology of the universe and growth of cosmic structure
   * Measure effects of dark energy, consistency of general relativity
      and the curvature of spacetime
   * Detection of primordial black holes

TIME

  Barycentric Dynamical Time ("TDB" or T_eph) output was requested. This
continuous coordinate time is equivalent to the relativistic proper time
of a clock at rest in a reference frame co-moving with the solar system
barycenter but outside the system's gravity well. It is the independent
variable in the solar system relativistic equations of motion.

  TDB runs at a uniform rate of one SI second per second and is independent
of irregularities in Earth's rotation.
